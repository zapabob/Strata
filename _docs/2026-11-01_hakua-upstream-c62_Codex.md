## Native fixture and checkout corrections after intake

The pinned-upload diagnostic was not accepted as a fix: r1 passed but r2 failed, and r3 was not run. Original-q8, ordinary-step-load and system-fence overlays all failed. No kernel variant was applied to production. Inspection found the independent cause candidate in kv_window_parity: dalloc queues default-stream cudaMemset operations for input/step/output buffers AFTER the earlier setup synchronization, then a cudaStreamNonBlocking stream begins using those buffers without an initialization dependency. The completion of cudaMalloc or another incidental API must not be assumed to order it. NVIDIA CUDA13.3 documents asynchronous host behavior for cudaMemset and the absence of legacy-stream synchronization for nonblocking streams: https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/archive/13.3.0/cuda-runtime-api/api-sync-behavior.html and https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/archive/13.3.0/cuda-runtime-api/stream-sync-behavior.html .
